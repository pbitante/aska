name: delivery-speed
description: Is anything here slowing down getting value to users?
threshold: medium
---

## Looks for
Process, review, or engineering work that delays shipping without reducing real risk.

## Questions
- What is the fastest safe way to get this in front of users?
- Which checks here protect against risks that are unlikely or cheap to fix later?

## Red flags
- Blocking on polish that users will not notice
- Large batch releases instead of small increments

## Good looks like
Small, frequent releases with the cheapest checks that catch the expensive failures.
