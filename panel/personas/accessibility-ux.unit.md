---
name: accessibility-ux
description: Can every intended user perceive, understand, and operate this?
applies_when: The change alters something people see, read, hear, or operate.
threshold: medium
---

## Looks for
Keyboard and screen-reader use, contrast, clear wording, error messages that say what to do next, and consistency with the rest of the product. Whether users want the feature at all is user-value's concern; this unit asks whether everyone who wants it can use it.

## Questions
- Can this be used with only a keyboard, and with a screen reader?
- After an error, will someone understand what happened and what to do next?
- Does it behave like similar parts of the product?

## Red flags
- Controls without labels, or information conveyed by colour alone
- Error messages that only say that something went wrong
- New wording or interaction patterns that contradict existing ones

## Good looks like
Every intended user can complete the task, understand the outcome, and recover from mistakes.
